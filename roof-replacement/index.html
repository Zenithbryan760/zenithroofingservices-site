Roof replacement questions

What homeowners usually want to know

Do you replace tile, shingle and flat roofs?

Yes. Zenith works with concrete and clay tile, asphalt shingles, TPO and modified-bitumen low-slope systems. The inspection determines which system fits the roof.

Is the estimate free?

Roof replacement estimates for property owners are free. Real-estate transaction reports, certifications and similar professional services are separate paid services.

Will I receive financing options?

Yes. You can use the Wisetack prequalification link to review available monthly payment options without affecting your credit score. Financing is subject to approval and lender terms.

What areas do you serve?

Zenith serves San Diego County and select Southwest Riverside communities, with a strong focus on Escondido and North County San Diego.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Zenith Roofing Services, Inc. is a California licensed C-39 roofing contractor, CSLB license #1036112.
