Roofing decisions for local properties

Look below the surface before choosing the scope.

Many Carmel Mountain Ranch homes have concrete tile assemblies where the tile can remain serviceable after the original underlayment and flashing details have aged.

01

Repair an isolated defect

A focused repair may be appropriate when the leak source is clear and the surrounding underlayment, tile and flashing remain serviceable.

02

Lift and relay reusable tile

When the tile has remaining value but the waterproofing below is aging, a lift and lay exposes the deck and renews the complete assembly beneath it.

03

Replace when the roof requires it

Complete replacement may be considered when the tile cannot be reliably reused or when deck, design or system conditions make a new roof the responsible path.

Frequently asked questions.

Does Zenith provide roofing service in Carmel Mountain Ranch?

Yes. Zenith provides roof repair, tile lift and lay, roof replacement, maintenance and related roofing services for Carmel Mountain Ranch properties and nearby San Diego communities.

Can the existing concrete roof tile be reused?

Often, yes. Serviceable concrete tile can be carefully lifted and staged while the deck, underlayment and flashing below it are repaired or replaced. Tile condition and replacement availability are evaluated for each project.

Why replace underlayment if the tile still looks good?

Concrete tile is the durable outer covering, but wind-driven rain can reach the underlayment below. That hidden layer and the flashing system provide the primary water-control path and can age before the tile itself.

Will Zenith document hidden conditions?

Yes. Important conditions and installation stages can be documented with photographs, including exposed decking, wood repairs, underlayment and flashing details before the tile is reset.
