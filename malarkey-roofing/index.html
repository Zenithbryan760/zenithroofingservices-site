What’s inside a Malarkey Highlander shingle?

Rubber in the asphalt core

Malarkey blends SBS, a synthetic rubber, into the asphalt that coats the shingle’s fiberglass mat. This lets the asphalt flex as the roof heats up and cools down. The NEX® blend also uses reclaimed tire rubber and recycled plastic.

How Malarkey makes NEX asphalt

How the roof reduces smog

Sunlight activates a coating on the 3M™ granules. At the shingle surface, it converts nitrogen oxides in the air, including nitrogen dioxide from vehicle exhaust, into water-soluble nitrate salts. Rain rinses those salts off the roof.

What Class 3 measures

Malarkey lists Highlander and Highlander Cool as Class 3 under UL 2218, a laboratory impact test. If hail resistance matters to you, compare that rating across the shingles on your estimates. Your insurance policy spells out coverage for storm damage.

What we found after tear-off

We removed the old roof down to the wood. Some sheathing had rotted, and the original plank deck had open joints and knot holes. A shingle nail driven into one of those openings has no wood to grip.

Under the shingles

The blue sheet in our photos is Roofnado StormTread XW synthetic underlayment. For a Malarkey system, Secure Start® Plus is a synthetic underlayment option. Smart Start® is the starter shingle at the edges. In valleys, where two roof slopes drain together, we use 401 Arctic Seal® peel-and-stick membrane.

The adjoining low-slope roof

Three layers of OmniSeal® on the low slope

Water moves more slowly across the low-slope section. We used a continuous membrane here and primed the metal drip edge so the self-adhering asphalt would bond to it.

  1. 410 OmniSeal BaseA mechanically fastened base anchors the system to the wood deck.
  2. 420 OmniSeal Base/PlyWe used this self-adhering sheet as the middle layer, bonding it to the base.
  3. 430 OmniSeal CapA granulated cap sheet protects the layers underneath and finishes the transition to the shingles.

What does the 50-year warranty cover?

For an eligible owner-occupied, detached single-family primary residence, Malarkey’s Certified Pro warranty provides 50 years of non-prorated coverage for qualifying manufacturing defects on Highlander and Highlander Cool. “Non-prorated” means the age of the shingles does not reduce the covered amount during that period.

The system requires a full tear-off, Malarkey shingles and at least three qualifying Malarkey accessories. We list the underlayment, starter and hip and ridge shingles in your proposal and submit the registration to Malarkey for acceptance. Malarkey confirmed the shingle registration for the San Diego project shown here.

Zenith provides separate written workmanship terms. For an eligible adjoining low-slope area, Malarkey’s Certified Pro terms list 17 years for the three-layer OmniSeal Base, Ply and Cap assembly, with coverage limited to replacement membrane materials for qualifying manufacturing defects.

Questions to ask before a San Diego reroof

How do Malarkey Highlander Cool and Owens Corning Duration COOL compare?

Both offer reflective architectural shingles in darker colors. Highlander Cool is worth a look if you like Malarkey’s SBS rubberized core and smog-reducing granules. Owens Corning’s TruDefinition® Duration® COOL features SureNail®, a woven strip that reinforces the fastening area and gives our installers a clear nailing target.

Zenith is a Malarkey Certified Pro Contractor and an Owens Corning Preferred Contractor. We can quote the same deck work and low-slope areas for either shingle so you can compare the installed prices, color samples and written warranties side by side.

What’s the difference between Highlander and Vista in Escondido?

Highlander and Highlander Cool carry a Class 3 impact rating. Vista® and Vista® Cool carry Class 4 on the same UL 2218 test. For 92026, Malarkey lists Vista in Sienna Blend and Silverwood, and Vista Cool in Moonlit Black, Riverstone Grey and Willow Wood. If you want the higher impact rating, we can quote Vista and check your color’s lead time.

Will a cool shingle make my attic cooler? How does Title 24 apply?

A reflective shingle can reduce heat absorbed at the roof surface. The change you feel indoors also depends on your insulation, attic ventilation and local climate. For a permit, we check the property address, roof slope and project type, then match the specific shingle color’s rating to the applicable California requirements.

What should my HOA see before I order a color?

Give the reviewer the manufacturer, exact shingle line and color, a physical sample if requested, and the California color sheet above. Names can repeat across product lines. For example, identify the proposed shingle as “Highlander Cool in Willow Wood” so everyone approves the same material.

What can change the cost of a Malarkey roof replacement?

Ask whether each quote includes tear-off and disposal, what it charges to replace damaged wood, and how it handles an adjoining low-slope roof. On the project above, replacing rotten sheathing and overlaying gapped planks were two different jobs. Ask for the wood-repair rates in writing before tear-off so you can price what the crew uncovers.

Can I transfer the Certified Pro warranty if I sell?

Malarkey allows one transfer to an eligible new owner. Notify Malarkey and complete its transfer application within 60 days of the property transfer. Keep the registration and proof of transfer. The time left on the original warranty determines the coverage available to the buyer.
