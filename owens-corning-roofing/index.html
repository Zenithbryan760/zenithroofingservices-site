Owens Corning Total Protection Roofing System

Owens Corning Total Protection works in layers.

Owens Corning describes the Total Protection Roofing System as an integrated group of components designed to help a roof perform in three critical areas. Zenith still verifies the deck, slope, transitions and attic before specifying the final assembly.

Seal

Help keep moisture away from the roof deck

  • Ice and water barrier at vulnerable areas
  • Synthetic underlayment beneath the shingles
Defend

Build the protective shingle assembly

  • Starter shingles at eaves and rakes
  • Field shingles plus matching hip and ridge shingles
Breathe

Plan balanced attic ventilation

  • Intake ventilation that allows outside air to enter
  • Exhaust ventilation that helps release heat and moisture

Important system distinction: Owens Corning states that non-Owens Corning items such as flashing, fasteners, pipe boots and wood decking are excluded from the branded Total Protection Roofing System. Zenith still inspects and specifies those roof-critical details separately. Read the official system overview.

California cool-roof guidance

Does Title 24 require a cool roof for my property?

It depends. For permit applications submitted on or after January 1, 2026, California's 2025 Energy Code applies. The correct path still depends on climate zone, building type, roof slope, project scope and applicable exceptions. A product name by itself does not settle compliance.

Standard, System Protection and Preferred Protection compared.

This plain-language comparison separates product-defect coverage, contractor-workmanship coverage and qualifying component requirements before the roof inspection.

Direct answer

What is the Owens Corning 50-year warranty? Owens Corning's current comparison lists a 50-year TRU PROtection period for qualifying System Protection and Preferred Protection roofing systems. Preferred Protection also lists workmanship coverage for the first 10 years. These are different coverage categories, and the actual warranty document controls.

What the 50-year period means

TRU PROtection is the initial enhanced coverage period.

Owens Corning explains that this initial period includes labor cost to repair or replace qualifying defective materials. The exact shingle, components, structure type, limitations and warranty terms determine coverage.

Direct answers before the estimate

Owens Corning roofing answers.

Clear, source-informed guidance reviewed for Zenith by Bryan Ramirez, roofing contractor and company principal. CSLB C-39 #1036112.

Is Zenith Roofing Services an Owens Corning Preferred Contractor?

Yes. Zenith Roofing Services is an Owens Corning Preferred Contractor and a California C-39 roofing contractor, CSLB #1036112. Enhanced manufacturer protection depends on the selected system, qualifying components, installation requirements and registration.

What does Owens Corning Preferred Contractor mean?

Owens Corning describes Preferred Contractors as independent contractors who meet its requirements for professionalism and reliability. Current program requirements include applicable licensing and insurance, at least $1 million in general liability insurance, and approved business credit. Preferred Contractors may offer eligible System Protection and Preferred Protection limited warranties.

What is the Owens Corning 50-year warranty?

Owens Corning's current warranty comparison lists a 50-year TRU PROtection period for qualifying System Protection and Preferred Protection roofing systems. Preferred Protection also lists workmanship coverage for the first 10 years. The actual warranty document, installed products and registration control coverage.

What is the difference between System Protection and Preferred Protection?

Both current extended options list a 50-year TRU PROtection period for qualifying systems. System Protection does not list workmanship coverage and generally requires Owens Corning hip and ridge shingles plus two other qualifying accessories. Preferred Protection lists 10 years of workmanship coverage and generally requires hip and ridge shingles, underlayment and two additional qualifying accessories.

What components are required for Preferred Protection?

Owens Corning currently identifies four qualifying roofing components for Preferred Protection: Owens Corning hip and ridge shingles, Owens Corning underlayment and two additional qualifying Owens Corning accessories. Eligibility must be confirmed for the specific roof and documented in the proposal.

Is the Total Protection Roofing System the same as a warranty?

No. Total Protection describes the integrated SEAL, DEFEND and BREATHE roofing-system approach. A warranty is a separate legal document with its own product, contractor, installation and registration requirements.

Which Owens Corning shingles does Zenith install?

Zenith installs Owens Corning Oakridge, TruDefinition Duration and Duration COOL architectural shingle systems when they fit the roof, design goals and applicable requirements. Product availability, color, roof slope, ventilation and accessories are confirmed in the proposal.

Does every California reroof require a Title 24 cool roof?

No single answer applies to every roof. The current energy-code path depends on permit date, climate zone, building type, roof slope, project scope and applicable exceptions. Zenith verifies the jurisdiction and specified product rating before recommending a cool-roof assembly.

Can Budget Your Roof provide a final roof price?

No. It provides a preliminary planning range. It cannot see concealed layers, damaged decking, flashing conditions, low-slope areas, ventilation or access constraints. A final Zenith proposal requires roof measurements and an inspection.

What is included in a complete Owens Corning roof system?

A complete roof is more than visible shingles. The specified assembly can include deck correction, underlayment, leak protection, drip edge, starter, field shingles, hip and ridge shingles, flashing and balanced ventilation. The proposal should list the exact components.

What information is needed to register a warranty?

Owens Corning currently lists the installation date, shingle type, shingle color and number of installed roofing squares among the information needed for registration. Zenith supplies applicable closeout documents for the warranty included in the contract.

Can an Owens Corning roof warranty transfer to a new owner?

Owens Corning currently states that transfer to the second owner must be initiated within 60 days of the real-estate transfer. Supporting documents and a fee may apply under the warranty in effect at installation.
