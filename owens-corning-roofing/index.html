California cool-roof guidance

Does Title 24 require a cool roof for my property?

It depends. For permit applications submitted on or after January 1, 2026, California's 2025 Energy Code applies. The correct path still depends on climate zone, building type, roof slope, project scope and applicable exceptions. A product name by itself does not settle compliance.

Direct answers before the estimate

Owens Corning roofing questions

Reviewed for Zenith by Bryan Ramirez, roofing contractor and company principal. CSLB C-39 #1036112.

Is Zenith Roofing Services an Owens Corning Preferred Contractor?

Yes. Zenith Roofing Services is an Owens Corning Preferred Contractor and a California C-39 roofing contractor, CSLB #1036112. Eligibility for enhanced manufacturer protection depends on the selected roof system, qualifying components, installation requirements and registration.

Which Owens Corning shingles does Zenith install?

Zenith installs Owens Corning architectural shingle systems, including Duration and Duration COOL options when they fit the roof, design goals and applicable requirements. Product availability, color, roof slope, ventilation and the complete accessory package are confirmed in the proposal.

Does every California reroof require a Title 24 cool roof?

No single answer applies to every roof. The current energy code path depends on the permit date, climate zone, building type, roof slope, project scope and applicable exceptions. Zenith verifies the jurisdiction and specified product rating before recommending a cool-roof assembly.

Can Budget Your Roof provide a final roof price?

The official tool can help organize budget expectations and choices, but it cannot see concealed layers, damaged decking, flashing conditions, ventilation or access constraints. A final Zenith proposal requires a roof-specific inspection and documented scope.

What is included in a complete Owens Corning roof system?

A complete roof is more than the visible shingles. The specified assembly can include deck correction, underlayment, leak protection, drip edge, starter shingles, field shingles, hip and ridge shingles, flashing and balanced ventilation. The exact components should be listed in the proposal.

What Owens Corning warranty will my roof receive?

Warranty eligibility depends on the selected products, qualifying system components, installation requirements and registration. Zenith identifies the included workmanship and manufacturer coverage in the proposal and supplies applicable closeout documents after completion.
