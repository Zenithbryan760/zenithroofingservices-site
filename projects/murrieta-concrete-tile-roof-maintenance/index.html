The project decision

The tile roof was serviceable and ready for complete maintenance.

Zenith reviewed the accessible roof surface and found broken and shifted concrete tiles, areas where the material below had become exposed, and aging sealant at roof penetrations and other applicable details.

The roof did not require a complete lift and lay or replacement. The correct scope was a whole-roof maintenance service: correct the tile field, replace broken pieces, reseal penetrations and maintain vulnerable ridge, valley and flashing details where needed.

What Zenith completed

Complete maintenance from tile field to penetrations.

The maintenance covered the accessible roof surface and the repairable defects found during the service.

The finished result

The existing tile roof stayed in service after complete maintenance.

Zenith corrected the repairable conditions across the accessible roof instead of turning maintenance into an unnecessary replacement. Broken and shifted tiles were addressed, roof penetrations were maintained and compatible sealant was renewed at applicable details.

  • Complete accessible roof maintenance performed
  • Broken concrete tiles replaced where needed
  • Shifted tiles reset to restore roof coverage
  • Applicable penetrations, ridge and valley details resealed
Homeowner lesson

Good maintenance addresses the roof as a system—not only the first visible broken tile.

On this roof, the responsible scope included the accessible tile field, penetrations and compatible sealant details. Maintenance can preserve a serviceable tile roof, but it is not a substitute for a lift and lay when underlayment failure is widespread.
