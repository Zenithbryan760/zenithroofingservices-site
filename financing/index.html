Roof financing questions

Does checking eligibility affect my credit score?

No. Wisetack states that checking available financing options uses a soft credit inquiry and does not affect your credit score. If you accept an offer, additional credit review may apply.

Can I finance a new roof?

Eligible customers may receive options for qualifying roof replacements and other roofing projects. Available amounts, APRs and terms depend on creditworthiness, the requested amount and participating lending partners.

Does Zenith offer roofing payment plans directly?

Financing options are provided through Wisetack lending partners, not directly by Zenith Roofing Services. Zenith provides the roofing estimate, contract and installation scope.

Can I check financing before receiving an estimate?

You may check eligibility first, but a prequalification is not a final project price. Zenith must inspect or evaluate the roof and prepare the applicable roofing proposal.

What areas does Zenith serve?

Zenith Roofing Services is based in Escondido and serves San Diego County, North County communities and selected surrounding Southern California areas. Project availability depends on location and scope.

What information will I receive?

If options are available, Wisetack will show the applicable payment amount, APR and term before you decide whether to proceed. Prequalification estimates are not guaranteed financing offers.
