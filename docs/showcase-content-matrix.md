## Confirmed material knowledge for service education

- Titanium UDL50 and Titanium 30/UDL30 synthetic underlayments
- Titanium PSU30 self-adhered underlayment at valleys, transitions, and other critical areas when specified
- Titanium FR assemblies when the approved roof design calls for a fire-rated underlayment
- Malarkey RightStart starter shingles
- Malarkey OmniSeal self-adhered roofing system
- Johns Manville DIBITEN APP modified-bitumen systems
- WeatherBond TPO systems, including fully adhered and mechanically attached assemblies selected to match the roof
- Uniflex silicone restoration systems

Materials must be tied to the actual approved roof assembly. The site should not imply that every material is used on every project.
