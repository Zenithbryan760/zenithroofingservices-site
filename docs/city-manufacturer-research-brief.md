# City Manufacturer and Roofing-System Research Brief

## Purpose

Research each existing Zenith service-area page before any city copy is written. The goal is to identify one genuinely useful, city-specific roofing answer and the manufacturer or system that best supports it. This is not a request to place every brand on every page.

The final city section should help a property owner answer a local question, support SEO and Answer Engine Optimization, and point to the appropriate central Zenith authority page. Do not create new city URLs or change an existing city page during the audit.

## Current code baseline

- For permit applications submitted on or after January 1, 2026, use the California 2025 Building Energy Efficiency Standards as the current statewide baseline.
- Record the building climate zone for the exact city or community. Do not assume every location in a county has the same climate zone.
- Record the permitting jurisdiction. An unincorporated community may use a county authority even when its mailing address names a nearby city.
- Treat cool-roof applicability as a project-specific determination. Building type, roof slope, project scope, climate zone, current product rating and exceptions can all matter.
- Confirm the exact product and color in the current CRRC Rated Roof Products Directory when a section makes a cool-roof claim. A directory listing is a rating, not a blanket code approval or guarantee of availability.

Primary statewide sources:

- California Energy Commission, 2025 standards: <https://www.energy.ca.gov/programs-and-topics/programs/building-energy-efficiency-standards/2025-building-energy-efficiency>
- California Energy Commission climate-zone tool: <https://www.energy.ca.gov/programs-and-topics/programs/building-energy-efficiency-standards/climate-zone-tool-maps-and>
- Cool Roof Rating Council directory: <https://coolroofs.org/directory/roof>

## Evidence hierarchy

Use sources in this order:

1. Official city, county, state or fire-authority pages and adopted code documents.
2. California Energy Commission and Cool Roof Rating Council records.
3. Official manufacturer product, warranty and installation documents.
4. Zenith project, inspection, estimate and CompanyCam evidence for that location.
5. Search-demand evidence from Google Search Console, Google Ads Keyword Planner or an approved SEO platform.
6. Reputable secondary sources only when no primary source answers a non-code factual question.

Do not cite a competitor, lead-generation directory or AI summary as authority for code, certification, warranty or product performance.

## AEO questions to answer

Select one or two questions per city based on evidence. Do not force the same question onto all 64 pages.

- Who installs Owens Corning shingles in `[CITY]`?
- Is Zenith an Owens Corning Preferred Contractor serving `[CITY]`?
- Does a reroof in `[CITY]` need a Title 24 cool-roof product?
- Which Owens Corning shingle fits homes in `[CITY]`?
- Is Duration COOL useful or required in `[CITY]`?
- What should an HOA in `[CITY]` compare before approving a shingle color?
- Are Malarkey Vista Cool or GAF Timberline HDZ RS alternatives for this property?
- Is a coating, TPO, PVC or shingle system appropriate for this roof slope?
- Can an existing commercial roof in `[CITY]` be restored with a UNIFLEX system?
- When does a WeatherBond TPO system make sense for a property in `[CITY]`?

Each answer must identify the entity, location and condition directly. A good answer begins with the conclusion, then gives the two or three variables that change it.

## Opportunity scoring

| Grade | Standard | Planned use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Official local source, exact product source, current rating when needed, and Zenith service evidence | Strong unique city section |
| B | Official local source and current manufacturer source, but limited Zenith city evidence | Short conditional city section |
| C | Search demand exists but local or product evidence is incomplete | Hold for more research |
| Skip | No meaningful local fit, duplicate intent or unsupported claim | Do not add the brand to that city page |

## Selection rule for each city page

Choose the one manufacturer or system with the strongest local evidence. Add a second only when it answers a different roof type or decision. Examples:

- A steep-slope HOA community may warrant Owens Corning color and Duration COOL guidance.
- A commercial corridor may warrant WeatherBond TPO or UNIFLEX restoration guidance.
- A hotter inland community may justify a carefully sourced cool-roof comparison among Owens Corning, Malarkey and GAF.
- A coastal neighborhood does not automatically justify a cool-roof claim. Use its actual climate zone, permitting authority and roof conditions.

Do not place Owens Corning, Malarkey, GAF, UNIFLEX and WeatherBond on all 64 pages. That would dilute the local answer and create repetitive copy.

## Required final output from the audit

For every service area, deliver:

1. Completed research record with live source links and access dates.
2. One primary keyword cluster and one question-form AEO cluster.
3. One 40 to 80 word direct-answer draft.
4. One recommended manufacturer or system, or `Skip`.
5. Exact internal link target.
6. Proposed section heading, title-tag modifier and meta-description angle.
7. Evidence grade and reviewer note.

The title tag should keep the page's main roofing intent first. A manufacturer modifier belongs in the title only when demand and page content support it. Do not turn every city title into a list of brands.

## Claims that require a stop and verification

- `Title 24 compliant` without the exact current product/color and project path.
- `Required by code` without current jurisdiction, climate-zone and project-scope support.
- `Preferred`, `certified`, `authorized`, `Master Elite` or similar contractor status without proof.
- Exact warranty years or coverage without the current warranty document and qualifying assembly.
- Product availability or color availability without a current source or supplier confirmation.
- Energy savings, roof-temperature reductions or insurance savings without a qualified source and careful limitations.
- Claims that a coating can restore a roof without inspection, moisture and adhesion findings.

## Review standard before city-page implementation

Bryan or the designated Zenith reviewer should approve the manufacturer selection, local answer, contractor-status wording and code language before the section is added to an existing city page. Preserve every city's current URL, primary service intent and useful local copy.
