More than new tile

The same roof-system discipline as lift & lay—built for all-new tile.

We remove the existing roof covering, inspect the deck and details exposed during tear-off, install the specified water-shedding layers and flashings, then install the new tile system. The exact scope depends on the roof and selected material, but the goal is always a complete, coordinated roof—not a surface change.

Replacement makes sense when

You want a fresh, consistent roof—not an aging mix of existing tile and repairs.

New tile can be the right choice when a roof has widespread breakage, difficult-to-match discontinued material, aged components below the tile, or when the homeowner wants an entirely new color and profile.

Plan the roof and solar together

Solar picture frames and clear access can be designed into a new tile roof.

On selected projects, we can plan a defined tile border or access space around solar areas. This can make future service access clearer, reduce unnecessary tile breakage from foot traffic, and create a cleaner visual transition around the panels.

It is a project-specific layout decision—not a one-size-fits-all detail. We coordinate the roof layout with the solar plan before the tile installation is finalized.
